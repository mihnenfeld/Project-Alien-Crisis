# PAC art direction

**Target:** industrial survival horror with believable sci-fi technology in a functioning-but-deteriorating research facility.

## Required
- Familiar industrial logic: service access, fixings, cables, ventilation, labels.
- Plausible proportions: door 2.3–2.7 m, large equipment at human scale.
- Materials: painted metal, steel, rubber, worn plastic, glass, grime. Rough PBR look.
- Lighting: practical sources, cold work-light, amber status light, restrained red emergency cues.
- Silhouettes readable through fixed, distant cinematic cameras.

## Reject as default
- Smooth sterile luxury spacecraft furniture.
- Glow-heavy cyberpunk or fantasy machines.
- Low-poly untextured replacements presented as finished models.
- Geometry that obscures required walkways or room transitions.

## Scene standard
- Node3D root at useful placement pivot
- Mesh child imported from licensed source
- Optional StaticBody3D/AnimatableBody3D/RigidBody3D depending on use
- Explicit collision shape and optional interaction Area3D
- Source metadata documented in inventory
